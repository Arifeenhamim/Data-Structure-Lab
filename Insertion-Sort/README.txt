INSERTION-SORT - PROBLEM DESCRIPTIONS

03_Insertion_Sort_Basic.cpp
Basic Insertion Sort: Accepts five numbers and sorts them in ascending order by inserting each element into its correct position in the sorted portion.

04_Insertion_Sort_Student_Marks_Practice.cpp
Student Marks Analysis: Sorts student marks and displays the highest, lowest, average, number of passed students (40 or above), and second-highest distinct mark.

05_Insertion_Sort_Product_Prices_Practice.cpp
Product Price Analysis: Sorts product prices and displays the cheapest, most expensive, average price, number of products below 500, and second-cheapest distinct price.
